dave-backup

A personal backup utility operated by David Harriman. It uses rclone to connect to Google Drive and restic to store encrypted backups in the owner's Google Drive account. It is for the owner's personal use and is not offered as a public service.

Privacy policy

Privacy policy

Effective October 18, 2026.

dave-backup requests Google Drive access limited to files it creates or files explicitly authorized for the app (the drive.file permission). This access is used to create, read, verify, restore, and manage the owner's backup files.

Backup contents are encrypted locally by restic before being uploaded to Google Drive. Google stores the encrypted backup data. The local backup software stores its Google authorization token on the owner's computer; the backup encryption passphrase is held in the owner's macOS Keychain.

This utility does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or share it with other users. It does not send backup contents to this website. Backup retention is managed by the owner; restoring files requires access to the encrypted repository and its encryption passphrase.

The owner may revoke the app's access through Google Account permissions. Revoking access stops future access by the app but does not itself delete existing backup files from Google Drive.

Questions about this personal utility may be directed through the owner's contact page.
