See the findings & systems questions

The Ohio Board of Pharmacy found that an etoposide order specified a 0.9% sodium chloride base, but the preparation was compounded with a 23.4% base. It also found incorrect labeling. The patient subsequently died.

My review lens: Examine ingredient access, preparation records, what the verifier could actually inspect, and the checks in effect. These are further review questions, not additional findings about this case.
