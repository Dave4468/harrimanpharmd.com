A clear starting point for your case

Consulting for plaintiff and defense counsel on medication errors, pediatric and neonatal pharmacotherapy, and hospital pharmacy practice. Each engagement begins with a defined question and scope.

Medication records review

Reconstruct the course of medication ordering, preparation, administration, and monitoring. Identify gaps in the record and questions that need further investigation.

Deliverables: A medication chronology, records gap list, and preliminary findings discussion or memo.

Pharmacy practice analysis

Examine dosing, order verification, sterile compounding, IV workflow, and medication safety processes in the context of the available records.

Deliverables: An analysis with record citations, relevant clinical references, and questions for counsel to explore.

Expert opinions and testimony

Separately scoped assessment of pharmacy standard of care and medication issues within my qualifications. Availability for deposition or trial is confirmed for each matter.

Deliverables: A written opinion or report and testimony, when supported by the evidence and appropriate to the engagement.

A preliminary consulting review is not a final expert opinion. Conclusions depend on the records and may identify limitations or evidence that does not support the proposed theory. Services are clinical and technical; I do not provide legal advice or representation.

How an engagement begins

The first conversation establishes fit, timing, and the question counsel needs answered.

  1. Inquiry and conflict screening

    Start with your firm, contact information, general service need, and deadline. We arrange conflict screening before substantive case information is shared.

  2. Scope and engagement

    We agree on the question, deliverable, budget, and schedule. Work begins after conflict clearance, a signed agreement, and receipt of the retainer.

  3. Secure review and delivery

    Records transfer takes place through an agreed secure channel. Findings distinguish what the records show, what remains uncertain, and what additional information is needed.
