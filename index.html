The analytical work

Understand the chart.
Examine the system.

A medical record spreads the story across orders, progress notes, laboratory results, administration records, and handoffs. I bring hospital pharmacy experience to connecting those pieces and explaining their significance to counsel.

01 / RECONSTRUCT

Make the record intelligible

Build a clinical chronology. Trace treatment decisions, medication exposure, monitoring, and changes in condition. Identify inconsistencies, missing records, and questions that warrant closer review.

02 / INVESTIGATE

Locate potential system failures

Examine how ordering, verification, preparation, administration, and escalation fit together. Compare documented practice with relevant policies and clinical references to identify possible breakdowns and contributing factors.

03 / EXPLAIN

Give counsel usable findings

Translate clinical complexity into clear explanations with record citations. Distinguish supported findings from open questions, identify further records to seek, and help counsel prepare focused questions for witnesses.

What you receive: A chronology, findings memo with record citations, records gap list, and a discussion of the clinical issues, tailored to the engagement.
