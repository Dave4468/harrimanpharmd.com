The work

A clear account of the evidence.
A careful explanation of its limits.

  1. 01

    Medical records review

    I connect orders, administration records, laboratory results, and clinical notes into a chronology. Findings identify the supporting record, unresolved discrepancies, and the information still needed.

  2. 02

    Hospital systems analysis

    I examine how work moved through the hospital: who had which information, what checks were available, and how concerns were escalated. My background includes root cause analyses and medication error reviews.

  3. 03

    Expert opinions & testimony

    I explain pharmacy practice and medication issues in plain language, with opinions tied to the evidence. Written reports and testimony are separately scoped to the questions and my qualifications.

Applications include medication injury and malpractice, sentinel events, and clinical questions in fraud, whistleblower, and patient safety retaliation matters. Legal conclusions remain with counsel.
